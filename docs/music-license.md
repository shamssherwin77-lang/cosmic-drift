## Provenance and changes

Audio editions were obtained from the openly licensed media in
[FitVita](https://github.com/smyrna-pergamum/fitvita), commit
`17ec1f80be03ec45747bc39a64b015491a6028fb`, files
`app/src/main/res/raw/med_01.ogg`, `med_07.ogg`, `med_09.ogg`, `med_10.ogg`.
The original project identifies Wikimedia Commons as its recording source and
credits all four editions to Kevin MacLeod under CC BY 3.0. It documents trimming,
loop fades, loudness normalization and Opus encoding. Cosmic Drift converted
these editions to 96 kbps stereo MP3 for browser compatibility and added metadata;
it did not add or generate any musical content. See `public/audio/CREDITS.txt`.

These are instrumental ambient/meditation recordings. No claim is made that
they are Buddhist chants or authentic recordings of a Buddhist ceremony.

## Player behavior

Music is streamed lazily from this site's own static files. No third-party player,
account, subscription or cross-origin stream is required. Two persistent media
players feed the existing independent music mixer, allowing a gentle crossfade
without decoding whole multi-minute recordings into JavaScript memory. Master
mute, music mute, journey pause and backgrounding pause the recording after a
short fade, preserving playback position. Load/play failures are displayed and a
selected track can be tapped again to retry. Engine and space remain independent.

The former procedural arrangements (Starlight Piano, Nebula Dreams, Quiet Orbit,
Lunar Tide) are no longer included in the current player. Their previous CC0
public-domain dedication remains in effect for copies of those former
arrangements; it does not apply to Kevin MacLeod's recordings.
