# Original relaxation music

The four new musical arrangements — **Starlight Piano**, **Nebula Dreams**,
**Quiet Orbit**, and **Lunar Tide** — were composed for Cosmic Drift. They use
original note sequences, chord progressions and synthesized instruments. No
third-party recordings, samples, streaming services or paid assets are used.

These four musical arrangements and their generated audio are dedicated to the
public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

They may be played, recorded, reused, modified and distributed for free, including
commercial use, without attribution. This dedication covers these new musical
arrangements and their generated audio; it does not change the licensing of the
rest of the application or its dependencies.

## Playback

The Audio settings panel mixes engine/Light Jump, space ambience and music
independently. Selecting a track begins playback after a user gesture and gently
crossfades the current track. The arrangements continue through a repeating
32-beat chord cycle; they are not short external audio-file loops. Only the
selected arrangement schedules notes, and at most one old track fades out.

Pause, master mute and backgrounding fade the output to silence. Resuming does
not queue missed notes. Track, channel toggles and levels are remembered locally;
master audio starts off after a reload to respect browser playback rules.
